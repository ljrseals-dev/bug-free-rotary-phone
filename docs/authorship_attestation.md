## 1. ICMJE authorship criteria

Every listed author must meet all four ICMJE criteria:

1. Substantial contributions to conception/design, or acquisition/analysis/interpretation of data.
2. Drafting the work or revising it critically for important intellectual content.
3. Final approval of the version to be published.
4. Agreement to be accountable for all aspects of the work.

A person who meets fewer than four criteria is acknowledged, not listed as an author.

## 4. Use of generative AI — disclosure

APA 7th edition requires that AI-generated content be cited and disclosed. The
reference format is: Author. (Year). *Name of model* (version) [Large language
model]. URL. This project used an AI assistant for
research-scaffolding, literature synthesis, and document drafting. That use must
be disclosed in the paper's methods or acknowledgements, not hidden.

**Proposed disclosure text for the paper:**

> **Disclosure of generative AI use.** An AI assistant was used to search
> literature, draft scaffolding documents, and propose candidate implementations.
> All substantive research decisions, ethical judgments, and authorship
> attestations were made by the human authors. The AI system is cited in the
> references per APA 7th edition guidance.

**Reference entry to include:**

> OpenAI. (2026). *ChatGPT* (GPT-4 class version) [Large language model].
> https://chat.openai.com/

*(Replace the model name and version with the exact tool used, and the access
date if the version is dated.)*
