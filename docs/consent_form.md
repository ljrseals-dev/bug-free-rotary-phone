## 1. What this study is about

We are studying two things: (a) whether synthetic speech can approximate a person's
self-perceived "inner voice," and (b) how little speech data is needed to create a
usable personalized synthetic voice.

## 2. What we will ask you to do

- Provide up to **10 minutes** of recorded speech in a quiet room.
- Complete a short demographic questionnaire (age band, gender, dialect region, height).
- Optionally, listen to synthesized samples and rate them.

## 3. What we will create

We will train a **small adapter** — a lightweight modification to an existing open-source
speech model — using your recordings. We will generate synthetic speech that sounds like you.

**We will not** create or distribute a standalone model of your voice.

## 4. How your data will be handled

- Raw audio: encrypted local storage, access limited to named researchers.
- Embeddings (numeric voice representations): treated as sensitive, never released.
- Retention: raw audio up to 24 months; embeddings until study end + 6 months.

## 5. What we will release

Choose **one** release tier:

- [ ] **Tier 1 (default)** — Research analysis only. No synthetic audio leaves the lab.
- [ ] **Tier 2** — Tier 1, plus consented synthetic demo audio included in the publication.
- [ ] **Tier 3** — Tier 2, plus release of the trained adapter (never a baked voice model).

## 6. Withdrawal

You may withdraw at any time, for any reason, with no penalty. On withdrawal:

- Raw audio and derived embeddings are deleted within **30 days**.
- Any adapter trained on your voice is deleted or retrained without your data.
- You will receive written confirmation of deletion.

## 7. Risks

- Synthetic speech resembling you could, in principle, be misused. We mitigate by not
 releasing voice models and by labeling all synthetic audio.
- Listening to a synthetic approximation of your own voice may be mildly unsettling.
 You may stop at any point.

## 8. Benefits

There is no direct benefit to you. Results may inform accessible speech technology and
responsible voice-AI practice.

## 9. Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question and stop any task.
